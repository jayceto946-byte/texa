## 分部积分推导

**第一次分部积分**：取 $u = x^2$，$dv = e^x\,dx$，则 $du = 2x\,dx$，$v = e^x$。

$$\int_0^1 x^2 e^x\,dx = \left[x^2 e^x\right]_0^1 - \int_0^1 2x e^x\,dx = e - 2\int_0^1 x e^x\,dx$$

**第二次分部积分**：对 $\int_0^1 x e^x\,dx$，取 $u = x$，$dv = e^x\,dx$，则 $du = dx$，$v = e^x$。

$$\int_0^1 x e^x\,dx = \left[x e^x\right]_0^1 - \int_0^1 e^x\,dx = e - \left[e^x\right]_0^1 = e - (e - 1) = 1$$

**代回**：

$$\int_0^1 x^2 e^x\,dx = e - 2 \times 1 = \boxed{e - 2}$$

$$F(1) - F(0) = e^1(1 - 2 + 2) - e^0(0 - 0 + 2) = e - 2$$
